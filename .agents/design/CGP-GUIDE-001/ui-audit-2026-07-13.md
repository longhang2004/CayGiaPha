# Prototype UI audit — guidance sync and early-access welcome

Date: 2026-07-13

## Scope

Captured `/prototype/home`, `/prototype/signin`, `/prototype/signup`,
`/prototype/tree`, `/prototype/tree/empty`, `/prototype/help`, and
`/prototype/tree-list?welcome=open&trees=none` at 1280×800, 768×1024, and
375×667. The early-access dialog was also exercised in dark mode and at 200%
text size.

## Result for this change

- No blocking UI regression was found on the changed homepage, Help guide, or
  early-access dialog.
- The homepage Help CTA remains visible without colliding with the session CTA;
  it stacks to a full-width control on mobile.
- The Help guide renders all 13 topics and its table-of-contents anchors reach
  the matching sections without horizontal overflow.
- The welcome dialog stays within all three viewports. Its header and footer
  remain fixed while the body can scroll, so the close control and “Tôi đã
  hiểu” remain reachable on mobile and at 200% text.
- Focus restoration, Escape dismissal, dark-theme colors, and horizontal
  overflow checks passed in Playwright.

## Existing baseline observations — outside this change

- `/prototype/signup` at tablet width shows dark side gutters around the auth
  shell. This was already outside the affected surfaces and should be confirmed
  against the intended auth-shell background before changing it.
- `/prototype/tree/empty` shows a dark navigation rail/band at desktop and
  responsive widths. It does not overlap the onboarding content, but its visual
  treatment differs sharply from the warm page background and merits a separate
  shell review.

## Optional polish — not applied

- The Help page is necessarily very long on mobile. A collapsible or sticky
  table of contents could shorten navigation, but that would be a separate UX
  change.
- The homepage title and top navigation labels wrap on the 375px viewport.
  They remain readable and do not overflow; any tighter mobile typography would
  be visual polish rather than a correctness fix.
