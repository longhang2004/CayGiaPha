# CGP-CONFORMANCE-001 UI audit — 2026-07-13

## Scope and method

- Captured 48 deterministic prototype screenshots with Playwright: 16 scenarios at
  1280×800, 768×1024, and 375×667.
- Covered home, sign-in, sign-up, password recovery, early-access welcome, empty tree,
  Owner/Contributor/Linked/Reader workspaces, claim, Help, settings/data rights,
  consent reacceptance, and both legal v2 documents.
- `tests/e2e/prototype-audit.spec.ts` also asserted that every route returned HTTP 200 and
  that document width never exceeded the viewport.
- Separately exercised welcome and consent dialogs in dark mode at mobile 200% text,
  including Escape dismissal and the consent reopen path.

## Findings before polish

| Severity | Finding | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Blocking bug | None. No clipped primary action, modal/toolbar collision, horizontal page overflow, or inaccessible role action was observed. | No remediation required. |
| Conformance | Owner sees content and administration actions; Contributor sees people/relationship/photo editing without administration; Linked sees own-node/photo actions without structure editing; Reader is read-only. | Conformant; retained as Playwright and component-test evidence. |
| Conformance | Welcome and consent dialogs remain inside the 375×667 viewport, preserve usable body/footer scrolling, and expose their required links/actions. | Conformant. |
| Polish | Help and legal documents are intentionally comprehensive and therefore produce long mobile pages. A future sticky section navigator or back-to-top affordance could reduce travel. | Outside this remediation scope; not changed. |
| Polish | On a small viewport, an expanded guidance card plus an open person panel leaves little graph area visible, although neither overlaps the toolbar or graph controls. | Outside this remediation scope; consider a later compact-guidance presentation. |
